## Instagram source

Direct automated retrieval was throttled during research, so the project should **not pretend we extracted every post**.

Recommended content workflow:
1. User/owner supplies or downloads 12–20 approved restaurant photos/reel cover images.
2. Store them locally under `src/assets/restaurant/`.
3. Optimize to WebP/AVIF.
4. Maintain a small `gallery.ts` content file with caption + optional Instagram post URL.

Do not hotlink Instagram CDN media in production.
